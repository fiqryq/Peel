Peel

Coffee Bag Journal for iPhone

Snap a coffee bag and Peel turns it into a sticker. Collect every bag you brew on monthly sticker sheets, note how it tasted, and share it to your Stories.

Support

Found a bug or have a question? Email peel@fiqry.dev and include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

Common questions

My bags don't show up on my other device.

Peel syncs through iCloud. On both devices, make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account and that iCloud is turned on for Peel in Settings → your name → iCloud. Syncing can take a minute.

I bought Peel Pro but it's locked.

In Peel, open Collector → Peel Pro and tap Restore Purchase. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account you bought it with.

The sticker cut out the wrong thing.

Photograph the bag on a plain surface with the whole bag in frame. You can also trim the sticker before saving it.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a bag in the app to remove it from every synced device. To remove everything from iCloud, go to Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Peel.
